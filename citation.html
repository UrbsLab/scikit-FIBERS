Citing scikit-FIBERS

If you use scikit-FIBERS in a scientific publication, please consider citing the following paper as well as noting the release applied within the manuscript.

The most recent release (Beta 2.1.0) is associated with a manuscript currently in preparation. Other scikit-FIBERS relevant citations are included below.

scikit-FIBERS Methods Papers

Evolutionary Feature-Binning with Adaptive Burden Thresholding for Biomedical Risk Stratification. Harsh Bandhey, Sphia Sadek, Malek Kamoun, Ryan J. Urbanowicz (2024).
